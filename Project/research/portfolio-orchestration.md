# Research-portfolio orchestration — OpenAI CDC prompt (read in full, 30 Aug 2026)

Source: the verbatim prompt behind GPT-5.6 Sol Ultra's Cycle Double Cover
proof (https://cdn.openai.com/pdf/04d1d1e4-bc75-476a-97cf-49055cd98d31/cdc_prompt.pdf).
Its power is PORTFOLIO MANAGEMENT, not agent count. Principles, verbatim
where it matters:

1. "Begin with a genuinely diverse portfolio of approaches" — Track 2's
   opening queue (three variants of ONE ranking-loss idea) violated this
   and burned the whole convergence allowance on one family.
2. "Do not tell most agents the currently favored approach. Preserve
   independence during early rounds" — blind independence against
   groupthink; matches our blind cross-family Sol reviews (reviewer-bias.md).
3. "Maintain an explicit registry of approach families. Group agents by the
   mathematical idea they are using, NOT by superficial wording" — adopt:
   two kernels sharing the same mechanism are ONE direction family sharing
   strikes; renaming an idea is not a new direction.
4. "A route that ends at a lemma equivalent in strength to the original
   conjecture is not close to completion" — anti-fake-progress: a change
   that just relocates the bottleneck is not a win unless the new
   bottleneck is measurably cheaper.
5. "When an approach stalls at a theorem-strength missing lemma, mark that
   route as blocked. Only continue assigning agents to it if someone
   proposes a materially new mechanism, invariant, or construction" —
   INDEPENDENT CONVERGENCE with our 3-strike closure + case-for-revival
   clause ("materially new mechanism" = our revival bar, verbatim spirit).
6. "Keep several incompatible proof routes alive through multiple rounds.
   Cross-pollinate only after independent agents have developed them far
   enough" — portfolio breadth before merging.
7. "Require agents to return concrete lemmas, constructions, equations, or
   counterexamples... Reject status reports, vague optimism, and claims
   that [something unproved] is 'routine'" — evidence-not-vibes reporting.
8. Adversarial agents get an EXPLICIT failure-class checklist (their list:
   exact-two multiplicity, trails masquerading as cycles, bridges
   introduced by reductions, circular use of an equivalent statement...) —
   adopt for our freeze-time review: each reviewer gets a NAMED defect
   class, not "find problems".
9. Root agent repeatedly synthesizes, challenges, redirects, relaunches.

DO NOT COPY (task-specific): assume-a-solution-exists framing; 8-hour
minimum; 64 agents; refusing partial/negative reports (our negatives are
evidence); banning research on the exact problem (our research is legal
and mandated). GPU evaluation stays serialized on a quiet box regardless.

Application adopted for Track 3 (pending owner go): the freeze-time
INDEPENDENT ADVERSARIAL FINAL REVIEW — several read-only reviewers, one
defect class each (rules/eligibility; evidence-vs-artifact binding; report
claims vs data; shape-6/14 evidence; reproduction), reporting
independently BEFORE any merge, findings combined into one list, changes
decided only after. Caveat: the proof itself was unverified at publication
(no peer review/Lean) — copy the orchestration, not the triumphalism.
